FAQ

The usual questions.

Are the cats real?

No. Every cat, every location and every shot is generated with AI video models. No animals are filmed, handled or involved in any way.

How is a film made?

A start frame is generated, then a motion prompt turns it into a few seconds of footage. Dozens of those short takes get cut together until one holds for fifteen seconds.

Where can I watch?

On @infinite.auras. New films go up regularly.
