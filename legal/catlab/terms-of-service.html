TikTok integration

The app connects to TikTok through TikTok’s official APIs (Login Kit and the Display API) using access you explicitly grant. It reads your account totals and the metrics of your own videos with the user.info.stats and video.list scopes. It is read-only: it never posts, edits or deletes content, and it cannot act on anyone else’s account. You can revoke access at any time from your TikTok account settings.
