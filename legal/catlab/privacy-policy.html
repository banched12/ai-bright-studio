Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 August 2026

catlab.exe (“the app”) is a personal desktop dashboard used by its owner to read the statistics of their own social accounts, including TikTok. This policy explains what the app does with data.

What we collect

The app is a single-user desktop tool. It does not operate a server, and it does not collect, sell, or share your personal data with third parties. When you connect a TikTok account, TikTok returns an access token, a refresh token, and your basic profile info (open id and display name via the user.info.basic scope). These are stored only on your own computer, in your local application-data folder (%APPDATA%\AutoPost).

How we use it

  • Authentication tokens are used solely to call the TikTok Display API on your behalf when you refresh your statistics.
  • Profile info (display name) is shown in the app only so you can confirm which account is connected.
  • Account and video statistics — follower and view totals, and per-video views, likes, comments and shares — are read with the user.info.stats and video.list scopes and cached on your computer so the charts still draw while offline.

What we do not do

The app does not post, edit or delete anything on TikTok. We do not track you, share data with advertisers, or transmit your tokens or statistics anywhere except to the official TikTok API endpoints required to read them.

Data retention & deletion

Tokens and the cached statistics remain on your computer until you disconnect the account in the app or delete the files in %APPDATA%\AutoPost (tiktok_stats.json and tiktok_stats_cache.json). You can revoke the app’s access at any time from your TikTok account settings.
