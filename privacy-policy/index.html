Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

Infinite Auras (“the app”) is a personal desktop application used by its owner to publish their own short videos to social platforms, including TikTok. This policy explains what the app does with data. This website (infiniteauras.com) is a static information page and does not collect any data from visitors.

What we collect

The app is a single-user desktop tool. It does not operate a server, and it does not collect, sell, or share your personal data with third parties. When you connect a TikTok account, TikTok returns an access token, a refresh token, and your basic profile info (open id and display name via the user.info.basic scope). These are stored only on your own computer, in your local application-data folder (%APPDATA%\AutoPost).

How we use it

  • Authentication tokens are used solely to call the TikTok Content Posting API on your behalf when you choose to post a video.
  • Profile info (display name) is shown in the app only so you can confirm which account is connected.
  • Videos you select are uploaded directly to TikTok using the video.publish scope, only when you explicitly click Post.

What we do not do

We do not track you, run analytics, share data with advertisers, or transmit your files or tokens anywhere except to the official TikTok API endpoints required to publish your post. This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

Data retention & deletion

Tokens remain on your computer until you disconnect the account in the app or delete the file %APPDATA%\AutoPost\tiktok.json. You can revoke the app’s access at any time from your TikTok account settings.
